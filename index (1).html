In short

The app has no accounts and we run no servers. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. The free version shows adverts through Google AdMob, and Google may process some data to show them, as explained below.

What stays on your device

Your settings, saved games (scores, players, roll history), profiles, custom dice and, if you enter one, your ANU Quantum Numbers key are kept only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Quantum random numbers

When you choose a quantum source, the app asks one of these services for random numbers over the internet:

  • LfD Regensburg (lfdr.de), a university laboratory at OTH Regensburg, Germany;
  • ANU Quantum Numbers (quantumnumbers.anu.edu.au), the Australian National University's service, only if you add your own key.

The request contains only how many numbers are needed (and your key, for ANU Quantum Numbers). As with any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address. Those services have their own privacy practices. With the device's own randomness selected, no such requests are made.

Adverts (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob to show adverts. To show and measure them, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with adverts.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised adverts are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

The paid version shows no adverts and loads no advertising code.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; Google tells the app only whether the purchase was made.

Permissions

The app uses internet access (for quantum numbers and adverts) and vibration (for haptic feedback). Text-to-speech, where used, runs on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for adverts, use the choices above or Google's tools. For any question, write to akexstrim@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
